# ALLOCATOR.AI Client Demonstration Playbook

Use this walkthrough script when presenting **ALLOCATOR.AI** to client executives, university deans, or technical stakeholders.

---

## 🎭 Demonstration Steps (5-Minute Script)

### Step 1: High-Impact Landing Page (1 Minute)
- **Action**: Open `http://localhost:3000`.
- **Script**: *"Welcome to ALLOCATOR.AI. This platform completely automates technical trainer matching for university bootcamps using an autonomous 7-agent AI system."*
- **Highlight**: Point out the live glowing agent activity feed and dark glassmorphic design.

### Step 2: Live AI Multi-Agent Allocation Engine (2 Minutes)
- **Action**: Click **"AI Allocation Engine"** or navigate to `/dashboard/matching`.
- **Action**: Click **"Run Multi-Agent Allocation Pipeline"**.
- **Script**: *"Here, Gemini 1.5 Pro agents analyze the college request parameters, cross-reference 4,800+ vector profiles in Supabase pgvector, verify real-time calendar clearance, and compute a 5-criteria match matrix."*
- **Highlight**: Point out the step-by-step progress visualizer, **Match Score Circles (96.4%)**, Explainable AI reasoning text, Key Strengths, and click **"+ Compare Candidate"** to show the side-by-side matrix.

### Step 3: Manager Approval & Digital Contract (1 Minute)
- **Action**: Click **"Confirm Trainer Allocation"** or navigate to `/dashboard/assignments`.
- **Action**: Click **"Review & Approve"**.
- **Script**: *"As an allocation manager, you review the risk assessment score. Once approved, the platform automatically generates a legally binding digital PDF contract (`CTR-2026-901`) and sends instant Email & WhatsApp notifications."*
- **Highlight**: Show the PDF contract viewer modal and downloadable agreement.

### Step 4: Executive Intelligence Platform (1 Minute)
- **Action**: Navigate to `/dashboard/analytics`.
- **Script**: *"Executives gain complete real-time visibility through Datadog-style infrastructure telemetry, Recharts domain demand visualizations, Gemini AI trend predictions, and one-click PDF/CSV report exports."*
- **Highlight**: Show the date filters, AI insight pills, and click **"Reset Demo"** in the top navbar if you wish to reset the demo state!
